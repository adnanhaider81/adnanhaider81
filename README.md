I am a **Research Scientist in the Department of Virology, National Institute of Health, Islamabad, Pakistan**, with more than ten years of experience connecting laboratory methods, sequencing, bioinformatics and genomic interpretation.

## Clinical and pathogen genomics

**Clinical genomics · Rehman Medical Institute · October 2015–January 2021**

As a Scientific Officer at the Center for Genomic Sciences, I worked on whole-exome and targeted-panel sequencing for inherited disorders and cancer. My responsibilities combined laboratory work, bioinformatic analysis, germline and somatic variant review, and clinician-facing reporting supported by reference databases and published literature. I contributed to research on thalassemia, familial deafness and oral cancer.

[Clinical experience](https://syedadnanhaider.com/about.html#clinical-genomics) · [Related publications](https://syedadnanhaider.com/publications.html#clinical-genomics)

**Pathogen genomics · NIH Islamabad · April 2021–present**

My work supports surveillance and outbreak investigations involving emerging, re-emerging and other priority pathogens, using clinical and environmental samples. It spans molecular biology, Illumina and Oxford Nanopore sequencing, metagenomics, comparative genomics, phylogenetics and public-health reporting. I develop documented analysis workflows and maintain Pakistan's public SARS-CoV-2 Nextstrain Community build.

[Pathogen genomics experience](https://syedadnanhaider.com/about.html#pathogen-genomics) · [Related publications](https://syedadnanhaider.com/publications.html#pathogen-genomics)

## Methods and practical work

- **Laboratory and sequencing:** DNA/RNA workflows, whole-exome and targeted-panel sequencing, amplicon and metagenomic libraries; Illumina, Oxford Nanopore and Ion Torrent.
- **Analysis and interpretation:** sequence-quality review, mapping, assembly, variant interpretation, consensus generation, comparative genomics and phylogenetics.
- **Reproducible workflows:** Linux, Python and R; documented pipelines using Nextstrain, Snakemake and Nextflow.
- **Reporting and training:** evidence-based interpretation, technical reports, standard operating procedures and practical bioinformatics teaching.
